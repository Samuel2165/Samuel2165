## Hi there 👋, I'm Samuel

I'm an industrial engineer and data scientist from Colombia 🇨🇴, currently living in France 🇫🇷 while pursuing a Master in Engineering – Data Science at [EURECOM](https://www.eurecom.fr/fr).

I like working at the intersection of **mathematical optimization**, **data analysis**, and **data visualization**: turning messy, complex, and often qualitative information into models, insights, and decisions that actually help people.

### 🔭 What I'm working on

Right now, I'm involved in a few projects:

- A project with [EURECOM](https://www.eurecom.fr/fr), where I'm doing my master's.
- Collaboration and academic projects with the [Technological University of Pereira](https://utp.edu.co).
- One of the most exciting projects on my workbench: optimizing the distribution of perishable food in Colombia using a **stochastic optimization model** 🤓.

I also enjoy building clear, interactive visualizations—especially when the underlying data is complex and needs a story, not just a chart.

### 🌱 What I'm learning

I'm currently deepening my knowledge in:

- Applied AI for small, specific, and practical tasks 🔨
- Local-first and privacy-friendly solutions
- Open-source tools, standards, and workflows
- Advanced optimization and decision-making under uncertainty

I'm particularly interested in AI applications that solve narrow, real-world problems well, rather than building general-purpose systems for the sake of it.

### 💡 Why I do this

For me, much of modern technology exists because of small contributions from people—often in academia—working on side projects: [GNU/Linux](https://www.gnu.org/software/software.html), [Git](https://git-scm.com/), [Signal](https://signal.org/), [Code - OSS / Visual Studio Code](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode), [Markdown](https://daringfireball.net/projects/markdown/), and [Python](https://www.python.org/).

That philosophy of contributing to shared knowledge, simply because it makes the world a little better, is something I deeply admire. If you have an interesting idea and think I could help, count on me 💪🏽.
